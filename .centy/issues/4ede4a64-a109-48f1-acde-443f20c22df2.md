# reassignment_count uses unchecked as-cast from usize to u32 in reconcile

In reconcile.rs line 160, reassignments.len() as u32 is an unchecked cast that could theoretically overflow with more than 4 billion items. Consider using u32::try_from or saturating conversion for consistency with the rest of the codebase’s arithmetic safety approach.
